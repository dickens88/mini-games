Privacy, in plain words

Last updated: September 2026

We don't collect anything

There are no accounts on this site. We never ask for a name, an email address, a photo, or an age. There is no chat, no comments and no way to upload anything.

Your scores stay on your device

Games remember your best time using your browser's own local storage. That data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Clearing your browser data erases it.

No ads and no tracking

There are no advertising scripts and no third-party trackers. We use privacy-friendly, cookie-free visitor counts (Cloudflare Web Analytics) that tell us how many people opened a page — never who they are.

Made for younger players

Because children play here, the site is deliberately built so there is nothing personal to collect in the first place. If you are a parent or teacher and something on the site worries you, please write to us and we will fix it.
